Add Wise as a payout method

Interactive prototype for existing Instagram creators who can't receive USD. Choose a payout currency first, and the payout methods update to match it, including local rails such as US ACH through Wise. Light and dark mode run side by side; each one keeps its own state.

Flow

  1. Professional dashboard → Payouts: a nudge says "You could be losing money on currency conversion".
  2. Education: why the bank can't receive USD, a $500 cost example, what Wise is, why new details are needed, and that it's reversible.
  3. Payout currency (step 1): defaults to INR from the creator's country and can be changed. The note below it updates for each currency.
  4. Payout method (step 2): options depend on the currency. USD gives Wise via US ACH, EUR gives SEPA, GBP gives Faster Payments, INR gives local bank or wire, and AED gives wire only.
  5. Do you have a Wise account? (step 3): use an existing account, or go to Wise to create one and come back.
  6. Account details (step 4): name and country are pre-filled and locked. The fields change with the payment rail and are checked as you type, with inline help.
  7. Verifying, with an optional name mismatch error. Then Review and confirm (step 5), then Success with a "Make default" switch, and back to Payouts with the new method showing.
